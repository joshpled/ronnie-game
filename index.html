WELCOME TO HER WORLD

A little care.
A lot of love.

Tap the floor to call Ronnie over, or tap her for a little affection. You can also use the buttons below her room.

  • Feed fills her bowl and restores fullness.
  • Pet gives her a happiness boost.
  • Play sends her chasing a ball. It uses a little energy.
  • Rest restores energy over time, even while you’re away. Tap Wake when she’s ready.

Her needs change gently. Time away is capped at eight hours, so there’s no rush to return.

Progress stays in this browser on this device. Clearing website data clears the save. This early prototype reuses her existing poses; dedicated eating and sleeping animations are next.
